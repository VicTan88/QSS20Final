A Machine Learning Analysis of Commercialization Within the #tcm Creator Economy

Rather than over-commercializing indefinitely, independent creators in the Traditional Chinese Medicine space strategically bound explicit business signals to protect audience trust and safeguard their social capital.

Victoria Tan

The Core Problem & Hypothesis

The Structural Trap

Social media data infrastructures enforce a rigid binary between personal curation and commercial intent. Standard machine learning evaluation pipelines systematically default to maximize overall raw accuracy, creating shortcuts that render niche minority subcultures structurally invisible due to major class imbalances.

Why It Matters

Tracking the viral "Chinamaxxing" trend on social platforms shows Western and Asian creators sharing global wellness traditions. Evaluating how digital subcommunities navigate corporate pressures is critical to understanding cross-cultural appropriation and preventing algorithmic erasure of niche communities.

The Non-Linear Process

Instead of maximizing monetization indefinitely, creators observe economic boundaries. We hypothesize a structural stabilization takes place over time where creators voluntarily curb explicit commercial jargon to insulate their audience from intrusive mass commercialism.

The Machine Learning Pipeline

1

Chronological Concatenation

Raw data slices were integrated sequentially and strictly filtered to isolate English posts, standardizing features for text vectorization pipelines.

2

Regex Tokenization

Advanced regular expressions isolated hashtags from caption blocks, partitioning variables cleanly into hashtags and text_clean tracking arrays.

3

Heuristic Target Labeling

Built regular expression patterns to map user intent into binary metrics: Organic Personal (0) vs. Commercial Intent (1) by detecting transactional triggers.

4

L1-Regularized Logistic Regression

Applied an L1-regularized model to accurately separate complex user profiles, enforcing parameter sparsity to track the tokens driving text commercialization.

5

Imbalance Optimization

Prioritized classification precision over raw accuracy to balance out the 88.6% organic baseline, scoring a 93.08% precision peak at optimized parameter limits.

Context Collapse Defense

When external corporate forces cross over into highly localized subcultures to acquire monetization equity, they introduce acute structural changes. Traditional subcommunities successfully adapt by systematically transforming language boundaries or modifying behavioral signals to safeguard niche solidarity.

The Future of NLP Modeling

Relying exclusively on surface-level lexicon extraction patterns completely misinterprets how swiftly creators self-regulate to bypass algorithms. Advanced classification networks must scale text processing models to track multi-modal variables (like direct image logo analysis) to fully unpack modern niche transaction systems.
